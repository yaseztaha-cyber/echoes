# References and credits

ECHOES combines mission-history research, planetary elevation data, spacecraft models, and archival media. The links below are the starting points for checking those materials. Mission-specific claims and asset credits should remain attached to the relevant record in the application; this page is a readable index, not a substitute for those in-context credits.

## Planetary terrain and mapping

- **Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter (MOLA) — NASA Planetary Data System, Geosciences Node.** MOLA measurements were used to produce Mars topographic profiles and global elevation products. ECHOES uses MOLA-derived terrain data for its Mars environment.  
  https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/mgs/mola.html
- **Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA) — NASA Science.** LOLA measures lunar elevation, surface roughness, reflectance, and slopes from the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter. ECHOES uses LOLA-derived terrain data for its lunar environment.  
  https://science.nasa.gov/mission/lro/lola/
- **USGS Astrogeology.** Planetary map products and basemaps used where identified in the asset register.  
  https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/

## Spacecraft and mission records

- **NASA 3D Resources — Mars Exploration Rovers.** Source for the MER rover model identified in the asset register.  
  https://science.nasa.gov/resource/mars-exploration-rovers-3d-model/
- **NASA Science — Mars Exploration Rovers: Spirit and Opportunity.** Mission background, rover objectives, instruments, and mission history.  
  https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-exploration-rovers-spirit-and-opportunity/
- **NASA Science — Mars Pathfinder.** Pathfinder and Sojourner mission background.  
  https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-pathfinder/
- **NASA Science — InSight.** Mission objectives, instruments, and end-of-mission information.  
  https://science.nasa.gov/mission/insight/
- **NASA Science — Voyager 1.** Mission history and current mission context.  
  https://science.nasa.gov/mission/voyager-1/
- **NASA — Voyager 1 Makes History Reaching One Light-Day From Earth.** One light-day milestone on 18 November 2026 at 2:16 a.m. PST (10:16 UTC); about 25.9 billion km / 16.1 billion mi; 24-hour one-way signal time.  
  https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasas-voyager-1-makes-history-reaching-one-light-day-from-earth/
- **NASA Science — Voyager 1 Engineers Analyze Data Issue, Ready to Confirm Light-day.** Dated status: no science or engineering data since 14 November 2026; carrier signal still detected; milestone confirmed from carrier signal and trajectory calculations.  
  https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/voyager/2026/11/18/voyager-1-engineers-analyze-data-issue-ready-to-confirm-light-day/
- **NASA NSSDCA Master Catalog.** Spacecraft records and mission identifiers; consult the individual record linked from the mission source register.  
  https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/

Apollo mission histories are linked individually from the mission source register and should be checked against NASA's Apollo mission pages and the Apollo Lunar Surface Journal:
- https://www.nasa.gov/history/
- https://www.nasa.gov/mission/

## Orbits and solar observations

- **JPL Solar System Dynamics — Approximate Positions of the Major Planets.** Approximate Keplerian elements used by the project's orbital model. These are an approximation, not a precision ephemeris.  
  https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/approx_pos.html
- **NASA Solar Dynamics Observatory (SDO).** Source for the solar imagery and instrument layers identified in the asset register.  
  https://sdo.gsfc.nasa.gov/

## Archival imagery and media

- **NASA Image and Video Library.** Searchable NASA mission photographs, video, and other media. Individual assets should retain their own creator, identifier, caption, and source-page credit.  
  https://images.nasa.gov/
- **NASA Scientific Visualization Studio.** NASA scientific visualizations and selected planetary visual material. Check the credit and usage notes for each item.  
  https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/
- **NASA Earth Observatory / Visible Earth.** Earth imagery and global mosaics where identified in the asset register.  
  https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/  
  https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/

## Attribution and interpretation

- NASA materials are not evidence of NASA endorsement. ECHOES is an independent student project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by NASA.
- A NASA-hosted asset may still have a third-party creator or usage restriction. Follow the credit and rights information attached to each individual asset.
- Artist-created textures, reconstructions, and visualizations are not direct observations. ECHOES should label them accordingly and preserve the licence and creator credit.
- Reconstructed spacecraft are not official NASA 3D models. The model source and reconstruction status are recorded in `ASSET_LICENSE_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md`.
- The full mission-by-mission claim trail is maintained in `MISSION_SOURCE_REGISTER.md`. The asset-by-asset credits and licence notes are maintained in `ASSET_LICENSE_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md`.
