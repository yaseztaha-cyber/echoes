### OPPORTUNITY (MER-B) — MISSION COMPLETE

- **Agency:** NASA / JPL-Caltech
- **Launch vehicle:** Delta II Heavy 7925H (7925)
- **Launch site:** Space Launch Complex 17B, Cape Canaveral Air Force Station, Florida
- **Objective:** Search for and characterise rocks and soils at a Martian site selected for evidence of ancient liquid water, using a mobile laboratory on the surface.
- **Status:** NASA ended attempts to contact Opportunity on 14 February 2019 after the last transmission on 10 June 2018; a dust storm ended the mission's power budget.
- **Hardware today:** At Perseverance Valley on the rim of Endeavour Crater; NASA's final traverse map places the rover there on 10 June 2018.

### SPIRIT (MER-A) — MISSION COMPLETE

- **Agency:** NASA / JPL-Caltech
- **Launch vehicle:** Delta II 7925
- **Launch site:** Space Launch Complex 17A, Cape Canaveral Air Force Station, Florida
- **Objective:** Search for evidence of past water activity at Gusev Crater and characterise the rocks and soils of the Columbia Hills.
- **Status:** The rover stopped responding in a 2010 global dust storm; NASA ended recovery efforts on 25 May 2011. This is a mission end, not a hardware abandonment.
- **Hardware today:** Still on Mars in Gusev Crater, at Troy (Columbia Hills).

### SOJOURNER (MPF) — MISSION COMPLETE

- **Agency:** NASA / JPL-Caltech
- **Launch vehicle:** Delta II 7925
- **Launch site:** Space Launch Complex 17A, Cape Canaveral Air Force Station, Florida
- **Objective:** Demonstrate a low-cost airbag landing and deliver the first wheels on Mars, characterising the rocks and soils of an ancient streambed.
- **Status:** NASA's own mission record gives End of Mission 27 September 1997, after 83 sols — far beyond the planned seven.
- **Hardware today:** Still on Mars in Ares Vallis, roughly 100 m from the Pathfinder lander.

### INSIGHT (INSIGHT) — MISSION COMPLETE

- **Agency:** NASA / JPL-Caltech, with DLR, CNES and ESA
- **Launch vehicle:** Atlas V 401
- **Launch site:** Space Launch Complex 3E, Vandenberg Air Force Base, California
- **Objective:** Study the deep interior and the crust of Mars — its core, mantle structure, crustal thickness and heat flow — using a seismometer and a heat-flow probe.
- **Status:** NASA's mission page records End of Mission 15 December 2022, after the final data transmission.
- **Hardware today:** Still on Mars in Elysium Planitia (4.50°N, 135.62°E).

### APOLLO 12 (A12) — MISSION COMPLETE

- **Agency:** NASA
- **Launch vehicle:** Saturn V SA-507
- **Launch site:** Launch Complex 39A, Kennedy Space Center, Florida
- **Objective:** Land two people on the Moon near the Surveyor 3 site, confirm the crewed landing system, and return an astronaut to the lunar surface for a second time.
- **Status:** The crew returned to Earth on 24 November 1969; the mission ended with splashdown.
- **Hardware today:** The LM (Intrepid) and the S-IVB remain near the Surveyor 3 site; the ascent stage is lost.

### APOLLO 14 (A14) — MISSION COMPLETE

### APOLLO 17 (A17) — MISSION COMPLETE

- **Agency:** NASA
- **Launch vehicle:** Saturn V SA-515
- **Launch site:** Launch Complex 39A, Kennedy Space Center, Florida
- **Objective:** Explore the Taurus-Littrow valley — a highland/mare boundary rich in stratified layers — and leave the last Apollo lunar surface experiments in operation.

- **Hardware today:** The Challenger LM, the ALSEP and the lunar rover remain at Taurus-Littrow; the ALSEP operated until 1977.

### VOYAGER 1 (VGR-1) — ONGOING

- **Agency:** NASA / JPL-Caltech
- **Launch vehicle:** Titan IIIE-Centaur
- **Launch site:** Space Launch Complex 41, Cape Canaveral Air Force Station, Florida
- **Objective:** Survey Jupiter and Saturn, then continue outward into interstellar space, returning planetary and interstellar measurements for as long as the spacecraft can transmit.
- **Status:** NASA: 'Voyager 1 crossed into interstellar space in August 2012 and continues to collect data.' The mission has not ended; the instrument set has been reduced to save power, which is a spacecraft state, not a mission state.
- **Hardware today:** Interstellar space, beyond the heliopause — the most distant human-made object.
