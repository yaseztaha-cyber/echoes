# ECHOES — Submission Abstract

Space exploration produces extraordinary machines, observations, and discoveries, but their stories are often scattered across photographs, mission pages, and technical archives. This can make it difficult for students and the public to connect a spacecraft with the world it explored, the work it performed, and the scientific knowledge it helped produce.

ECHOES is a browser-based, interactive 3D solar-system experience designed to bring those connections together. Visitors can navigate between planetary bodies, descend to selected lunar and Martian mission sites, inspect spacecraft representations, and explore structured mission records. These records connect mission objectives, instruments, historical milestones, discoveries, and operational status to source references. The experience combines planetary terrain informed by MOLA and LOLA elevation data, JPL orbital elements, spacecraft models and reconstructions, and credited mission imagery.

ECHOES currently includes ten registered mission vehicles or spacecraft representations. Its Opportunity (MER-B) experience is the most developed example: alongside its chapter-based mission record, it includes a spatial waypoint route and an embedded 3D documentary. Other missions are presented through chapter-based records; they do not currently have equivalent spatial routes, and most do not yet include archival imagery.

By placing mission hardware and its scientific record within an explorable planetary context, ECHOES aims to make robotic and human space exploration easier to understand, investigate, and remember.
