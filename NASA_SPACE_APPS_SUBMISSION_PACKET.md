## What ECHOES does

ECHOES is a browser-based 3D exploration experience. Visitors can move through a navigable solar system, explore selected lunar and Martian environments, inspect ten registered mission vehicles or spacecraft representations, and open mission records about their objectives, instruments, milestones, discoveries, and status.

Opportunity (MER-B) currently has the most developed journey: a chapter-based story, a spatial waypoint route, and an embedded 3D documentary. The other mission journeys are chapter-based. Archival media coverage is not yet complete across all missions.

## Data and research

The project uses or references:
- MOLA-derived elevation data for Mars terrain.
- LOLA-derived elevation data for lunar terrain.
- JPL approximate Keplerian elements for the orbital model.
- NASA mission records and the NSSDCA Master Catalog for mission research.
- NASA's published Mars Exploration Rover 3D model for the MER rover representation identified in the asset register.
- NASA Image and Video Library and other individually credited media.

## Important eligibility check

The NASA Space Apps Global Organizing Team has clarified that Global Awards eligibility requires meaningful use of at least one NASA data source listed in the Resources section of the selected challenge. Merely citing NASA facts, mission pages, or photographs is not enough.

Before submitting, the team must:
1. Open the official [challenge Resources tab](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/nasas-discarded-equipment-storytelling/?tab=resources).
2. Identify which dataset in that list is actually used by ECHOES.
3. Point to the relevant implementation or data asset and explain what the dataset contributes.
4. If no used dataset matches, do not claim that the Global Awards data requirement has been met.

The current project references MOLA and LOLA terrain data, but this note does **not** assert that either one appears in the selected challenge's official Resources list.
